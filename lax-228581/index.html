      Abstract

      For every natural number kkk, there is a finite simple graph GGG whose treewidth tw(G)\mathrm{tw}(G)tw(G) and twin-width tww(G)\mathrm{tww}(G)tww(G) satisfy

      tw(G)≤2k+4and2k<tww(G).\mathrm{tw}(G) \le 2k + 4 \qquad\text{and}\qquad 2^k < \mathrm{tww}(G).tw(G)≤2k+4and2k<tww(G).

      Natural Language Statement

      Theorem

      ⊕P and PP are closed under complement. A first kernel is paired with a kernel that always holds, which adds one witness: a number is even exactly when its successor is odd, and c≤dc \le dc≤d exactly when c<d+1c < d + 1c<d+1, with the two counts swapped. Whether C=_==​P is closed under complement is open.

      Abstract

      We prove that binary-encoded CNF satisfiability is NP-complete under polynomial many-one reductions. The proof implements a SAT verifier and a reduction from bounded computations through Boolean circuits and gate clauses. It includes correctness, termination, and polynomial running-time bounds for the machines. Machine classes come from lax-434930.

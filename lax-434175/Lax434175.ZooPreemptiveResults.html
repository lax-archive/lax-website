      Definition

      The 95 results of The Scheduling Zoo on its preemptive problems on parallel machines that are stated here, each as a proposition about a reading of the problem (Results.PReadingResults.PReadingResults.PReading; for a family, Results.ReadingResults.ReadingResults.Reading at the problem's specification): its words of instances and schedules and what an online scheduler sees. Each names its source; where the proposition is weaker than the wording, a note says what is left out.

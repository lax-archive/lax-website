      Natural Language Statement

      Lemma

      On one common reference law, parity and all small coordinate-slice tests are incompatible once the synthetic identity exceeds the sum of local ranks. Separate empirical density caps and variance bounds transfer every test. Their total prediction and comparison errors must then be at least one. This is a conditional finite transfer theorem; constructing the actual synthetic reference law and its empirical test couplings remains necessary.

      Natural Language Statement

      Lemma

      Sections of the observed baseline pairings compress derivative outputs inside their original perpendicular value spaces. The two actual derivative families retain their barred domain quotients, preserve the whole response, and have rank at most 3K+28 in an actual recipe solution. The residual matrix rank, pure correction and final channel factorization remain separate.

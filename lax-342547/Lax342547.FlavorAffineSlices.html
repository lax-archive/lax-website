      Lemma

      Lemma 9.4 uses independent uniform affine coefficients on exactly the actual retained coordinates of the chosen flavor. The free projection is exactly uniform for generic, shared-only and every pure flavor. Conditioning on all prescribed component Grams has the explicit beta^-2 cost, with no cost for setting excluded flavor coordinates to zero. Arbitrary bounded common tests of the actual primal image arrays satisfy the checked variance budget.

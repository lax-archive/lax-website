      Abstract

      This submission formalizes basic notions for finite-lifetime temporal graphs, including timed vertex visits, strict temporal walks, concatenation, and time-bounded reachability. It also proves that, while the current reachable set is proper, extending a reachability interval by one connected snapshot reaches at least one additional vertex.

      The Lean formalization and proof development were carried out with substantial assistance from OpenAI GPT-5.6 through Codex.

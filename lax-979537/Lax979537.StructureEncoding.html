      Definition and theorem

      Encode a structure of size nnn by nnn one-bits and a zero-bit, followed by the characteristic tables of its relations in vocabulary order. Each table lists tuples in lexicographic order. The distinguished order is determined by nnn and needs no table. Append each coordinate aia_iai​ of the query tuple as aia_iai​ one-bits and a zero-bit.

      The fixed vocabulary and query arity determine all table boundaries. The encoding is injective and has length n+1+∑R∈σnarity⁡(R)+∑i<k(ai+1)n+1+\sum_{R\in\sigma}n^{\operatorname{arity}(R)}+\sum_{i<k}(a_i+1)n+1+∑R∈σ​narity(R)+∑i<k​(ai​+1). In particular, the length is at least nnn and polynomial in nnn for a fixed vocabulary and arity, including empty vocabularies and small universes.

      27 | 0 => [Fin.elim0]

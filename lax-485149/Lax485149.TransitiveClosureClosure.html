      Natural Language Statement

      Theorem

      FO(TC) definability travels backward along first-order reductions and along ordered first-order reductions: if PPP reduces to an FO(TC) definable problem, then PPP is FO(TC) definable, a walk on the interpreted structure being a walk on the base structure with the tags carried in the modes. It reads a problem on its finite instances only.

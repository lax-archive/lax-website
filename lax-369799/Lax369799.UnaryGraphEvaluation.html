      Theorem

      A finite unary graph generated by its initial vertex evaluates like any lasso obtained by cutting its path at a repeated vertex and composing the two nonempty words. Stable factorization proves that its value is accepted exactly when its unfolded word satisfies the algebraic omega condition.

      Abstract

      A verified Lean core for Lax validation

      Lax records proofs that may depend on statements not yet proved. To determine which statements are established, it must follow these dependencies without treating circular reasoning as a proof. We implement a validation core in Lean and prove its main guarantees.

      The core finds every axiom used by a declaration, checks the mathematical validation rules, and computes exactly the statements that follow from the available proofs and declared assumptions. We also verify a certificate checker: an accepted certificate accounts for every derivable statement, and every well-formed network has such a certificate with at most one step per statement. A controller requires matching reports of successful replay and inspection for the same identified artifact, and any failed stage prevents approval.

      The main theorem shows that every unconditional proved label returned by the combined entry point is sound under an explicit contract for Lean's kernel and faithful inspection. All 26 theorem statements have kernel-replayed proofs, with their dependencies recorded in the proof network. These guarantees concern the executable Lean functions defined here. Using them in the live Lax service still requires integration with source acquisition, input extraction, authentic runner reports, execution, and publication.

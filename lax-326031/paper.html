  4. defLax153141.GraphParameters

    Graph parameters and functional equivalence

    A graph parameter assigns a natural number to every finite simple graph. Two graph parameters p and q are functionally equivalent when each is bounded by a numerical function of the other: there are functions f, g : ℕ → ℕ such that every finite simple graph G satisfies both p(G) ≤ f(q(G)) and q(G) ≤ g(p(G)). Functionally equivalent parameters are bounded on exactly the same classes of graphs.

    Merge-Width

    A merge sequence of a finite simple graph consists of a coarsening sequence of partitions and a monotone sequence of graphs of resolved pairs. At each step, adjacency is uniform on the unresolved pairs between any two parts. The radius-rrr width of a merge sequence is the maximum number of parts of the preceding partition met by a radius-rrr ball in the graph of resolved pairs. The radius-rrr merge-width of a graph is the minimum width of a merge sequence of that graph. A graph class has bounded merge-width if these parameters are bounded by a function of rrr throughout the class.

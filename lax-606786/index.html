      Abstract

      A formalisation of the semi-invertible multiplicative ergodic theorem for strongly measurable, forward-integrable cocycles L\mathcal LL of bounded operators on a separable Banach space XXX, over an invertible ergodic transformation of a Lebesgue probability space. No invertibility or injectivity of the operators Lω\mathcal L_\omegaLω​ is assumed.

      The Lyapunov exponents are defined through the growth rates χk\chi_kχk​ of the Bernstein numbers of the iterates Lω(n)\mathcal L^{(n)}_\omegaLω(n)​, and the distinct exponents λ1>λ2>⋯\lambda_1 > \lambda_2 > \cdotsλ1​>λ2​>⋯ are read off from them. The main result is the Oseledets decomposition: for each lll the space splits almost surely as X=E1(ω)⊕⋯⊕El−1(ω)⊕Vl(ω)X = E_1(\omega) \oplus \cdots \oplus E_{l-1}(\omega) \oplus V_l(\omega)X=E1​(ω)⊕⋯⊕El−1​(ω)⊕Vl​(ω) into equivariant, measurable, finite-dimensional fast spaces Ei(ω)E_i(\omega)Ei​(ω), on which the cocycle grows at rate exactly λi\lambda_iλi​ both in norm and in slowest growth, and an invariant slow space Vl(ω)={x:λω(x)≤λl}V_l(\omega) = \{x : \lambda_\omega(x) \le \lambda_l\}Vl​(ω)={x:λω​(x)≤λl​}, with strongly measurable, tempered projections. The decomposition is nontrivial exactly when the cocycle is quasicompact.

      The submission also proves that the decomposition is unique, that the sum of the first iii fast spaces is exactly the set of vectors admitting a backward history decaying at rate λi\lambda_iλi​, and that the index of compactness lim⁡kχk\lim_k \chi_klimk​χk​ agrees with the growth rate of the distance of Lω(n)\mathcal L^{(n)}_\omegaLω(n)​ to the compact operators, so that quasicompactness can be read off from that growth rate. The proofs include a version of Kingman's subadditive ergodic theorem for balanced time intervals, and Birkhoff's ergodic theorem.

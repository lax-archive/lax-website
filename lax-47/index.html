Tight Inapproximability of Max Independent Set in Triangle-Free Graphs

lax-47·formalized by Édouard Bonnet @EdouardBonnet·created 2026-08-07·GitHub @091f414·Lean v4.30.0 · mathlib c5ea00351c28

Abstract

This submission formalizes the main inapproximability theorem of Tight Inapproximability of Max Independent Set in Triangle-Free Graphs. Assuming the Håstad–Zuckerman promise hardness of Max Independent Set, it proves that, for every constant ε>0\varepsilon>0ε>0, a polynomial-time N1/2−εN^{1/2-\varepsilon}N1/2−ε-approximation algorithm on NNN-vertex triangle-free graphs implies NP⊆BPPNP\subseteq BPPNP⊆BPP.

The reduction blows each vertex of an nnn-vertex graph up into nnn vertices, samples the blow-up edges independently, and resamples the edges of present triangles. Its termination and output-distribution estimates use the kernel-checked Moser–Tardos and Haeupler–Saha–Srinivasan proofs from submission Lax41. The formalization proves triangle-freeness, completeness, the fixed-set soundness estimate, and the final gap arithmetic. Computations carry an explicit number of elementary steps; polynomial time, NPNPNP, and BPPBPPBPP are defined directly through polynomial bounds on those step counts, without choosing a machine model.

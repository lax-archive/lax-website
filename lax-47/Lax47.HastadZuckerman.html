Theorem

For every constant δ∈(0,1/2)\delta\in(0,1/2)δ∈(0,1/2), it is NPNPNP-hard to distinguish an nnn-vertex graph HHH with α(H)≤nδ\alpha(H)\le n^\deltaα(H)≤nδ from one with α(H)≥n1−δ\alpha(H)\ge n^{1-\delta}α(H)≥n1−δ. This is the promise version of Zuckerman's Theorem 1.1 on graph complements, strengthening Håstad's result.

The Lean statement records precisely the computational consequence of that promise hardness: a bounded-error polynomial-step solver for the general-graph promise gap implies NP⊆BPPNP\subseteq BPPNP⊆BPP. It contains no triangle-free reduction and no conclusion from the present paper.

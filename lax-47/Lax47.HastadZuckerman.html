Theorem

For every constant δ∈(0,1/2)δ ∈ (0, 1/2)δ∈(0,1/2), it is NP-hard to distinguish an nnn-vertex graph with independence number at most nδn^δnδ from one with independence number at least n(1−δ)n^(1-δ)n(1−δ) (the promise version of Zuckerman's Theorem 1.1 on graph complements, strengthening Håstad's result).

Here the promise theorem is stated in the transfer form used by the paper: if the randomized expected-polynomial-step triangle-free blow-up reduction has been certified, then any polynomial-time N(1/2−ε)N^(1/2-ε)N(1/2−ε) approximation algorithm for triangle-free Max Independent Set yields a bounded-error randomized polynomial-time algorithm for the hard gap problem, and hence NP⊆BPPNP ⊆ BPPNP⊆BPP. The elementary choice δ=2εδ = 2εδ=2ε, cutoff, amplification, and finite exceptional inputs are included in this standard promise-hardness transfer so that the only unproved concept is precisely the Håstad–Zuckerman complexity result.

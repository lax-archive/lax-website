      Natural Language Statement

      Theorem

      Two finite linear orders with at least 2n2^n2n elements each are nnn-round equivalent, as structures over the vocabulary of the order. The duplicator maintains that the distances between pebbled points, the two ends of the order included, are equal up to truncation at a threshold that halves at each round.

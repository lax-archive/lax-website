      Abstract

      We formalize the complexity class P\mathrm{P}P and prove that it is closed under complement. We prove that the stack-machine and elementary single-tape definitions coincide, with polynomial bounds covering input conversion and output cleanup. Requiring finite work-stack alphabets leaves the class unchanged.

      This is the Mathlib 4.33.0 port of lax-554803.

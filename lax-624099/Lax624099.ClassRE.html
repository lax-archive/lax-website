      Natural Language Statement

      Definition

      RE is the class of decision problems definable in existential second-order logic with value invention, with the cofinal hardness of the NP core: a problem is RE-hard when every problem it reduces to, by a relativized ordered first-order reduction, is reduced to by every problem of RE. The complement of a decision problem has the no-instances of the problem as yes-instances, and co-RE is the class of problems whose complement is in RE. RE-completeness is membership together with RE-hardness.

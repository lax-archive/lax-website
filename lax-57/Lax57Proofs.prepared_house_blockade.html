Description

Apply the semisparse-blockade theorem at scale L=Q4dL=Q^{4d}L=Q4d and sample each block to a common size. Simultaneous cleaning retains at least half of every sample and makes each noncomplete pair sparse in both directions. In each cleaned block, either the complement has a large connected component, or its components yield the required complete QQQ-blockade. Choosing a large component in every block preserves the relations between blocks.

Attribution

Claim 7.1.1 of Nguyen, Scott, and Seymour, with reciprocal parameters and rounding expressed over the natural numbers.

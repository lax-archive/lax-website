Theorem

Deciding a first-order sentence is fixed-parameter tractable on every nowhere dense class of graphs, in almost linear time on a word random access machine: for every nowhere dense class, every sentence φ and every ε > 0 there are one program, one constant c and one time bound T with T(x) ≤ c · (|x| + 1)^(1+ε), such that at every word length w, on every member of the class given in compressed sparse row form as a word x each of whose entries v satisfies c(|x| + v + 1) ≤ 2^w, the program halts within T(x) steps and writes 111 if φ holds in the graph and 000 if it does not.

This is the theorem of Grohe, Kreutzer and Siebertz (JACM 2017), with the algorithm realized on the word RAM of Lax13. Nowhere denseness is the exact limit of this kind of tractability on monotone classes, and the input is the graph alone: unlike the Courcelle theorem of Lax11, which is handed a k-expression alongside the graph, every auxiliary object the algorithm consumes — orderings, neighborhood covers, splitter moves, distance profiles — is computed from the input. That computation is the main algorithmic weight of the theorem.

32
33The statement follows the house pattern of Lax11's Courcelle axiom:
34program and constant after the class data, the sentence and ε; before
35the graph and the word length; inputs restricted to encodings whose
36entries fit the word length under the same `c * (x.length + v + 1) ≤
372 ^ w` side condition; output by a classical `if` on satisfaction.
38The differences are the ones the theorem is about. The hypothesis is
39`Lax12.NowhereDense` verbatim — the endorsed shallow-minor form, not a
40restatement. The input predicate is `Lax11.EncodesGraph` alone: no
41expression, no ordering, no promise beyond membership in the class.
42The time bound cannot be the Courcelle form `c * (x.length + 1)`,
43since `n^(1+ε)` has no elementary spelling over ℕ: the bound function
44`T : List ℕ → ℕ` is existentially quantified and pinned by the
45real-valued side condition `(T x : ℝ) ≤ c * ((x.length : ℝ) + 1) ^
46(1 + ε)` — the same real-exponent idiom as Lax12's subpolynomial
47bounds, whose `^` is `Real.rpow`.
48
49The sentence ranges over plain first-order logic on graphs, `FO 0` of
50this submission — adjacency and equality, no colors: colors and
51distance atoms are working machinery of the proof, and surfacing them
52in the headline would weaken it (a statement over colored graphs
53follows by the standard encoding of colors as input, and the distance
54logic's headline role is played by the locality theorem concept). The
55parameter dependence f(φ, ε, C) of the fixed-parameter claim lives in
56the existential `c` and `T`, as in the source; the class enters
57through the choice of `c` and `T` only, the program depending on
58finitely many of its excluded-minor thresholds.

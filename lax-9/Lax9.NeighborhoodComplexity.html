      Definition

      For a finite simple graph GGG and a natural number ppp, the neighbourhood complexity πG(p)π_G(p)πG​(p) is the maximum, over sets XXX of ppp vertices, of the number of distinct traces on XXX of the neighbourhoods of vertices outside XXX. A graph class has linear neighbourhood complexity if πG(p)π_G(p)πG​(p) is bounded by a constant multiple of ppp for every positive ppp.

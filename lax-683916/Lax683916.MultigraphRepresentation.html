      Theorem

      Every simple loopless multigraph is a simple graph up to simultaneous isomorphism of its actual vertex and edge sets. Incidence is preserved: an edge has endpoints uuu and vvv precisely when its image is the unordered edge joining the images of uuu and vvv.

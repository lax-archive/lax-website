Proof of `Nowhere dense classes are uniformly quasi-wide`

Description

Every nowhere dense graph class is uniformly quasi-wide: for every radius rrr there are a threshold function NNN and a separator bound sss such that in every member, every vertex set of size at least NmN mNm contains a distance-rrr independent subset of size at least mmm after deleting at most sss vertices.

Proof strategy

The ported development proves the statement for classes indexed by arbitrary finite vertex types. The submitted class CCC is therefore closed under subgraph copies — the closure is nowhere dense in the ported sense as soon as CCC is nowhere dense in the submitted sense, since a ported minor model of a clique in a copy pushes forward to a submitted minor model in the host — the ported theorem is applied to the closure, and the conclusion is specialized back to the members of CCC themselves. What remains is packaging: the ported statement is phrased with FinsetFinsetFinset and cardcardcard, the submitted one with SetSetSet and Set.ncardSet.ncardSet.ncard, and the two are matched by Set.toFinsetSet.toFinsetSet.toFinset and Set.ncardcoefinsetSet.ncard_coe_finsetSet.ncardc​oef​inset. Distance independence and vertex deletion need no translation at all: the ported step-reduction modules are stated over the definitions of the submitted concept.

The ported induction alternates an odd and an even distance-reduction step and consumes Ramsey's theorem twice — directly, to extract a homogeneous set, and through the bipartite Ramsey lemma (Lemma 3.9 of the notes) — so both Ramsey statements of the finite−ramseyfinite-ramseyfinite−ramsey submission are assumed rather than reproved here.

Attribution

The statement is Lemma 3.4 of Chapter 4 of the sparsity lecture notes of Pilipczuk and Siebertz (numbering of the 2019/20 edition), the hard direction of their Theorem 3.2.

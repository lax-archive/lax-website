Description

Self-contained proof of the Bonnet–Déprés exponential gap: for every kkk, the Bonnet–Déprés graph BDkBD_kBDk​ has treewidth at most 2∗k+42*k + 42∗k+4 while its twin-width exceeds 2k2^k2k.

Proof strategy

The source development phrases contraction sequences as trigraph state machines; the proof bridges them to the submitted partition-based sequences through the invariant that black and red edges agree with completeness and non-homogeneity in the graph, and relabels the result onto the canonical vertex type FinnFin nFinn.

Attribution

Ported from Édouard Bonnet's formalization of Bonnet–Déprés, Twin-width can be exponential in treewidth (JCTB 2023).

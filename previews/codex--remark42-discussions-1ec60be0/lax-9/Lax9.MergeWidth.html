        Definition

        A merge sequence of a finite simple graph consists of a coarsening sequence of partitions and a monotone sequence of graphs of resolved pairs. At each step, adjacency is uniform on the unresolved pairs between any two parts. The radius-rrr width of a merge sequence is the maximum number of parts of the preceding partition met by a radius-rrr ball in the graph of resolved pairs. The radius-rrr merge-width of a graph is the minimum width of a merge sequence of that graph. A graph class has bounded merge-width if these parameters are bounded by a function of rrr throughout the class.

        47structure MergeSeq (G : SimpleGraph V) where
        48 /-- The number mmm of steps of the sequence. -/
        49 length : ℕ
        50 /-- The sequence is nonempty. -/
        51 one_le_length : 1 ≤ length
        52 /-- The partition PiPᵢPi​ at step iii (as a setoid on the vertices). -/
        53 part : ℕ → Setoid V
        54 /-- The graph (V,Ri)(V, Rᵢ)(V,Ri​) of resolved pairs at step iii. -/
        55 resolved : ℕ → SimpleGraph V
        56 /-- P1P₁P1​ is the partition into singletons. -/
        57 part_one : part 1 = ⊥
        58 /-- PmP_mPm​ is the trivial partition with a single part. -/
        59 part_length : part length = ⊤
        60 /-- The partitions get coarser. -/
        61 part_mono : ∀ ⦃i j⦄, 1 ≤ i → i ≤ j → j ≤ length → part i ≤ part j
        62 /-- The sets of resolved pairs are monotone. -/
        63 resolved_mono : ∀ ⦃i j⦄, 1 ≤ i → i ≤ j → j ≤ length → resolved i ≤ resolved j
        64 /-- Uniformity: unresolved pairs between two parts are all edges or all
        65 non-edges. -/
        66 uniform : ∀ ⦃i⦄, 1 ≤ i → i ≤ length → ∀ ⦃x x' y y' : V⦄,
        67 (part i).r x x' → (part i).r y y' → x ≠ y → x' ≠ y' →
        68 ¬ (resolved i).Adj x y → ¬ (resolved i).Adj x' y' →
        69 (G.Adj x y ↔ G.Adj x' y')

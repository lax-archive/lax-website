      Open question

      The P versus NP problem asks whether every language with polynomially bounded, polynomial-time verifiable certificates can also be decided in deterministic polynomial time. We state the conjectured separation P≠NP\mathrm{P} \ne \mathrm{NP}P=NP as an open question.

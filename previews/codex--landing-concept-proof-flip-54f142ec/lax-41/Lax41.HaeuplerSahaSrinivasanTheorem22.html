Theorem

Suppose the asymmetric local-lemma conditions hold for a finite set A\mathcal AA of bad events. Let BBB be any event determined by the same independent random variables, whether or not BBB belongs to A\mathcal AA. The probability that BBB is true at least once during the Moser–Tardos algorithm is at most

Pr⁡[B]∏C∈Γ(B)(1−x(C))−1.\Pr[B]\prod_{C\in\Gamma(B)}(1-x(C))^{-1}.Pr[B]C∈Γ(B)∏​(1−x(C))−1.

In particular, the same bound holds for the probability that BBB is true in the output distribution of the algorithm.

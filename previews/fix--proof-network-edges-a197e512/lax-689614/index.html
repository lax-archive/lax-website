      Abstract

      Arc Kayles is the normal-play game in which two players alternately remove the endpoints of an edge of a finite undirected graph. We state its PSPACE\mathrm{PSPACE}PSPACE-completeness under polynomial-time many-one reductions. The reduction is from the positive CNF game. Its main structural ingredient is the Sprague–Grundy formula g(a,b)=(a+b) mod 2+2(min⁡(a,b) mod 2)g(a,b)=(a+b)\bmod 2+2(\min(a,b)\bmod 2)g(a,b)=(a+b)mod2+2(min(a,b)mod2) for a biclique Ka,bK_{a,b}Ka,b​ with an independent set of additional vertices, where every biclique vertex has a pendant neighbor in that independent set.

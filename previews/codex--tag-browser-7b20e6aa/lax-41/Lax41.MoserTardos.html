6title: The Moser–Tardos theorem
7type: theorem
8---
9Let a finite family of mutually independent random variables determine a
10finite family of measurable bad events. For a bad event $A$, let $\Gamma(A)$
11be the other bad events sharing a variable with $A$. If there are numbers
12$x(A)\in(0,1)$ such that
13$$
14 \Pr[A] \le x(A)\prod_{B\in\Gamma(A)}(1-x(B)),
15$$
16then some assignment avoids every bad event. Moreover, the Moser--Tardos
17resampling algorithm resamples each $A$ at most an expected
18$x(A)/(1-x(A))$ times, so its expected total number of resamplings is at most
19the sum of these bounds.
20-/

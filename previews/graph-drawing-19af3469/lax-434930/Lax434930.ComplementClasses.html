      Definition

      For a class C\mathcal CC of binary languages, coC\mathrm{co}\mathcal CcoC consists of languages whose complements belong to C\mathcal CC. Complements are taken among all finite binary strings. In particular, A∈coNLA\in\mathrm{coNL}A∈coNL means A‾∈NL\overline A\in\mathrm{NL}A∈NL, and A∈coNPA\in\mathrm{coNP}A∈coNP means A‾∈NP\overline A\in\mathrm{NP}A∈NP. This operation complements each language; it does not take the set-theoretic complement of the class of languages.

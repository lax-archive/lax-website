      Definition

      A finite relational vocabulary is a finite list of relation arities. A structure has universe FinnFin nFinn and an interpretation of each relation. The numbering is used for binary input encodings; it is not available as an order predicate in the logic. Properties are required to be invariant under isomorphism, so they do not depend on the numbering.

      The representation and dense binary encoding are reused from the Immerman–Vardi submission. Here we forget its distinguished order. Empty universes and nullary relations are allowed.

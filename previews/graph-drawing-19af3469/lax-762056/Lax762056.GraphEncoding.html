      Definition

      An instance consists of a finite simple graph on {0,…,n−1}\{0,\ldots,n-1\}{0,…,n−1} and a threshold kkk. Its encoding is 1n01^n01n0, followed by the n2n^2n2 adjacency-matrix bits in row order, followed by 1k01^k01k0. Polynomial running time is measured in the length of this word.

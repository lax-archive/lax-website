      Definition

      A type-to-type function f:A→Bf : A \to Bf:A→B is regular under string representation (Definition C.5.2 of Transducers) if there is a regular string-to-string function f′f'f′ over the eight-letter alphabet making the square commute: f′f'f′ maps the representation of aaa to the representation of f(a)f(a)f(a), for every a∈Aa \in Aa∈A. Rational functions between types are defined in the same way. Nothing is required of f′f'f′ on the strings that represent no element.

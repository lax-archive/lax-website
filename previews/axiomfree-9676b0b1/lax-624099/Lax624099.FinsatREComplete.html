      Natural Language Statement

      Theorem

      FINSAT is RE-complete: it is definable in existential second-order logic with value invention, and every problem of RE reduces to it by an ordered first-order reduction, the generic reduction that writes the definition of a problem as a sentence whose finite models are its certificates. This is the logical form of Trakhtenbrot's theorem.

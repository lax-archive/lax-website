Erdős–Hajnal for the five-vertex path

      Abstract

      This submission formalizes Nguyen, Scott, and Seymour's proof that the five-vertex path P5P_5P5​ has the Erdős–Hajnal property. It proves that there is a positive integer qqq such that every finite graph GGG with no induced copy of P5P_5P5​ satisfies

      ∣V(G)∣≤max⁡{α(G),ω(G)}q.|V(G)| \leq \max\{\alpha(G),\omega(G)\}^{q}.∣V(G)∣≤max{α(G),ω(G)}q.

      The formalization follows the paper's blockade argument through polynomial semisparse blockades in house-free graphs, the sparse-house trichotomy and its iteration, and a final critical-graph argument. Density inequalities are stated over the natural numbers with denominators cleared. The development uses Rödl's theorem, sparse thinning, maximum-degree reduction, and the bipartite comb lemma from Lax 54; every argument specific to P5P_5P5​ and its complement is proved in this submission.

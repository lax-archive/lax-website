    Erdős–Hajnal theorem for the five-cycle

    The five-cycle has the Erdős–Hajnal property. Equivalently, there is a positive integer qqq such that every finite graph GGG with no induced five-cycle satisfies ∣V(G)∣≤max⁡{α(G),ω(G)}q|V(G)|\leq \max\{\alpha(G),\omega(G)\}^q∣V(G)∣≤max{α(G),ω(G)}q.

    Finite graph notions for the five-cycle Erdős–Hajnal theorem

The graph C5C_5C5​ is the cycle on five vertices, and induced-C5C_5C5​-freeness is expressed using mathlib's induced-containment relation.

      Natural Language Statement

      Definition

      Two problems built on a problem sss of the family with operations, as used for interval scheduling with eligible machine sets (lax-767944).

      A job is kept by a schedule if it is on time, for an objective that counts late jobs, or just in time, for one that counts jobs not just in time; for the other objectives no job is kept.

      At most kkk scheduled jobs. An instance is an instance xxx of sss together with a number kkk. A schedule is a schedule of xxx together with a set SSS of at most kkk jobs, the jobs it schedules; it is feasible if the schedule is feasible for sss. Its cost is the weight of the jobs that are not in SSS or not kept, so that a cost of at most DDD says that some schedule of at most kkk jobs keeps weight at least W−DW - DW−D, with WWW the total weight. This is the weighted question parameterized by the number kkk of scheduled jobs: a schedule of at most kkk jobs and weight at least a target.

      Prescribed machine loads. An instance is an instance xxx of sss together with a number qiq_iqi​ for every machine iii. A schedule is feasible if it is feasible for sss and every machine iii runs exactly qiq_iqi​ jobs, a job running on a machine if a piece of one of its operations occupies it. The cost is the cost in sss; with the threshold 000 and an objective that counts jobs not just in time, the question is whether all jobs can be scheduled with these loads (Das, Opler and Valla).

      37variable (s : Spec OperationsFamily.family)

      39/-- The weight of a job. -/
      40def weight (x : Inst OperationsFamily.family s) (j : Fin x.base.jobs) : ℕ :=
      41 Objectives.weight (K := Operations.kernel) s.objective x.objectiveData j
      42
      43/-- The due date of a job. -/
      44def due (x : Inst OperationsFamily.family s) (j : Fin x.base.jobs) : ℕ :=
      45 Objectives.dueDate (K := Operations.kernel) s.objective x.objectiveData j
      46
      47/-- The job is kept: on time for an objective counting late jobs, just in
      48time for one counting jobs not just in time, and never otherwise. -/
      49def Kept (x : Inst OperationsFamily.family s) (σ : Operations.Sched x.base)
      50 (j : Fin x.base.jobs) : Prop :=
      51 match Objectives.kind s.objective with
      52 | .late => Operations.kernel.completion x.base σ j ≤ (due s x j : NNRat)
      53 | .notJustInTime => Operations.kernel.completion x.base σ j = (due s x j : NNRat)
      54 | _ => False
      55
      56open Classical in
      57/-- At most `k` scheduled jobs: the instance carries `k`, a schedule names the
      58at most `k` jobs it schedules, and the cost is the weight of the jobs not
      59scheduled or not kept. -/
      60noncomputable def atMostScheduled : Problem where
      61 Inst := Inst OperationsFamily.family s × ℕ
      62 Sched x := Operations.Sched x.1.base × Finset (Fin x.1.base.jobs)
      63 feasible x σ := (problem _ s).feasible x.1 σ.1 ∧ σ.2.card ≤ x.2
      64 cost x σ := ∑ j, (weight s x.1 j : ℚ) * if j ∈ σ.2 ∧ Kept s x.1 σ.1 j then 0 else 1
      65
      66/-- The jobs that run on a machine. -/
      67def jobsOn (x : Inst OperationsFamily.family s) (σ : Operations.Sched x.base)
      68 (i : Fin x.base.machines) : Set (Fin x.base.jobs) :=
      69 {j | ∃ k, Operations.Occupies σ ⟨j, k⟩ i}
      70
      71/-- Prescribed machine loads: the instance carries a number of jobs for every
      72machine, and a feasible schedule runs exactly that many jobs on every machine. -/
      73def prescribedLoads : Problem where
      74 Inst := (x : Inst OperationsFamily.family s) × (Fin x.base.machines → ℕ)
      75 Sched x := Operations.Sched x.1.base
      76 feasible x σ := (problem _ s).feasible x.1 σ ∧
      77 ∀ i, Set.ncard (jobsOn s x.1 σ i) = x.2 i
      78 cost x σ := (problem _ s).cost x.1 σ

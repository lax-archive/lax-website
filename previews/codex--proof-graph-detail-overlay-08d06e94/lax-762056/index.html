      Abstract

      Maximum Independent Set is NP-hard on graphs excluding the 5×55\times55×5 grid as an induced minor. A polynomial-time reduction from Max Cut maps a graph FFF to a graph GFG_FGF​ in this class such that α(GF)=MaxCut⁡(F)+2∑uv∈E(F), u<v(v−u)\alpha(G_F)=\operatorname{MaxCut}(F)+2\sum_{uv\in E(F),\,u<v}(v-u)α(GF​)=MaxCut(F)+2∑uv∈E(F),u<v​(v−u). Exclusion of the induced grid minor follows from an obstruction involving an asteroidal triple of cycles.

      This contradicts the polynomial-time conjecture of Dallard–Milanič–Štorgel and its weighted induced-subgraph generalization unless P=NP\mathrm{P}=\mathrm{NP}P=NP, and the quasipolynomial-time conjecture of Gartland–Lokshtanov and Korhonen unless NP⊆QP\mathrm{NP}\subseteq\mathrm{QP}NP⊆QP.

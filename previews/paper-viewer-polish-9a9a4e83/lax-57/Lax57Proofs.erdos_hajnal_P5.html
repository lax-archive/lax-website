Description

Apply the structural dichotomy to the complement of a P5P_5P5​-free graph. Strong induction reduces the proof to a critical graph. A large restricted set and a uniform blockade each contradict criticality, the latter by summing the blockwise values of κ\kappaκ. Finally, κ(G)≤max⁡{α(G),ω(G)}2\kappa(G)\leq \max\{\alpha(G),\omega(G)\}^2κ(G)≤max{α(G),ω(G)}2 gives the stated Erdős–Hajnal bound.

Attribution

The structural input is Lemma 7.3 of Nguyen, Scott, and Seymour. The critical-graph argument is an integral reformulation specific to this formalization.

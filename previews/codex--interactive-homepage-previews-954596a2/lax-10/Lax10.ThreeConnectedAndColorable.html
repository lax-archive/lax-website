3-connected graphs and 4-colorability

Definition

A graph is 333-connected when it has at least 4 vertices and remains connected after deleting any set of at most two vertices. A graph is mmm-colorable when its vertices have a proper coloring with at most mmm colors; in particular, 444-colorability is the case m=4m = 4m=4.

3/-!

5title: 3-connected graphs and 4-colorability
6type: definition
7---
8A graph is $3$-connected when it has at least 4 vertices and remains
9connected after deleting any set of at most two vertices. A graph is
10$m$-colorable when its vertices have a proper coloring with at most $m$
11colors; in particular, $4$-colorability is the case $m = 4$.
12-/
13
14open Finset
15
16namespace Lax10.ThreeConnectedAndColorable
17
18universe u
19
20variable {V : Type u}
21
22/-- A simple graph is colorable with at most $m$ colors. -/
23def KColorable (m : Nat) (G : SimpleGraph V) : Prop :=
24 G.Colorable m
25
26/-- The induced graph obtained after deleting the vertices in $S$. -/
27def deleteVertices (G : SimpleGraph V) (S : Finset V) :
28 SimpleGraph {v : V // v ∉ S} :=
29 G.induce {v : V | v ∉ S}
30
31/-- The set $S$ separates two vertices that remain after its deletion. -/
32def IsVertexSeparator (G : SimpleGraph V) (S : Finset V) : Prop :=
33 ∃ x y : {v : V // v ∉ S}, ¬ (deleteVertices G S).Reachable x y
34
35/--
36A graph is $3$-connected when it has at least 4 vertices and no vertex
37separator of size at most two.
38-/
39def ThreeConnected (G : SimpleGraph V) : Prop :=
40 4 ≤ Nat.card V ∧
41 ∀ S : Finset V, S.card ≤ 2 → ¬ IsVertexSeparator G S
42
43end Lax10.ThreeConnectedAndColorable

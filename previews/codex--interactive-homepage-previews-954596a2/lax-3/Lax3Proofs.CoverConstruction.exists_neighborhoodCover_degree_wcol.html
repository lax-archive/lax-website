Description

Neighborhood covers of weak coloring degree (Theorem 6.2 of Grohe–Kreutzer–Siebertz, via their Lemma 6.9): every graph has, for every radius rrr, an rrr-neighborhood cover of radius 2r2r2r whose degree is at most its weak 2r2r2r-coloring number.

Proof strategy

Take an ordering πππ attaining wcolG(2r)wcol G (2r)wcolG(2r) — the defining infimum is over a nonempty set of bounds, so it is attained — and let the cluster of uuu be w∣u∈wreachGπ(2r)w{w | u ∈ wreach G π (2r) w}w∣u∈wreachGπ(2r)w, the set of vertices from which uuu is weakly 2r2r2r-reachable.

The degree bound is the definition read backwards: the set of clusters containing vvv is indexed by u∣u∈wreachGπ(2r)v{u | u ∈ wreach G π (2r) v}u∣u∈wreachGπ(2r)v, which is wreachGπ(2r)vwreach G π (2r) vwreachGπ(2r)v itself, of size at most wcolG(2r)wcol G (2r)wcolG(2r) by the choice of πππ. The radius bound drops the minimality clause: a vertex in the cluster of uuu reaches uuu by a walk of length at most 2r2r2r, which reversed puts it in the 2r2r2r-ball of uuu.

Covering is the only real argument. Given vvv, let uuu be a πππ-minimal vertex of the rrr-ball of vvv, which is nonempty and finite. For www in that ball, concatenate the reversed ball walk w→vw → vw→v with the ball walk v→uv → uv→u: a walk of length at most 2r2r2r from www to uuu. Every vertex on it lies on one of the two halves, and cutting a walk of length at most rrr at any of its vertices shows that vertex to be within distance rrr of both endpoints — so the whole support stays inside the rrr-ball of vvv, where uuu is πππ-minimal. Hence uuu is weakly 2r2r2r-reachable from www, i.e. www lies in the cluster of uuu.

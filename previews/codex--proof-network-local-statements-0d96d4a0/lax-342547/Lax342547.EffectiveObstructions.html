      Lemma

      The actual two derivative tests force both residual contractions to zero. Double annihilation gives row and column membership in pin-plus-key spaces. Unselected sparse support removes the keys. Linear retractions identify the remaining row/column constraints with tensor-space membership, giving the actual effective remainder after selected subtraction.

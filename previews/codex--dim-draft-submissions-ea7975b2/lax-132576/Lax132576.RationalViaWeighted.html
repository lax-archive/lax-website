      Theorem

      A string-to-string function is rational if and only if weighted automata, over every semiring, are closed under pre-composition with it (Theorem B.3.6 of Transducers). The implication ⇒ is Lemma B.3.5 (WeightedPrecompositionWeightedPrecompositionWeightedPrecomposition) and the implication ⇐ is RationalOfWeightedPrecompositionRationalOfWeightedPrecompositionRationalOfWeightedPrecomposition; this statement is their conjunction.

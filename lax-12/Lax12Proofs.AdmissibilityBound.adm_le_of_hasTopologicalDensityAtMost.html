Description

If every depth-rrr topological minor of a graph GGG has at most ddd times as many edges as vertices, then the (r+1)(r+1)(r+1)-admissibility of GGG is at most 1+6∗(r+1)∗d31 + 6 * (r+1) * d ^ 31+6∗(r+1)∗d3.

Proof strategy

The ported development proves exactly this statement, at radius r+1r + 1r+1 with its hypothesis at depth (r+1)−1=r(r + 1) - 1 = r(r+1)−1=r, so the discharge is pure idiom translation and no weakening step is needed.

Two translations are involved. On the hypothesis side, the ported topological minor model routes each edge of the minor along a path, with a chosen tail and Sym2Sym2Sym2 plumbing, while the submitted model carries a walk for each adjacent pair; the repackaging orients an edge by its chosen tail and reverses the walk for the other orientation. The submitted density predicate ranges over minors on the canonical carriers FinmFin mFinm only, so the arbitrary finite carrier of the ported hypothesis is transported along Fintype.equivFinFintype.equivFinFintype.equivFin and the edge counts are matched by Set.ncardSet.ncardSet.ncard-to-edgeFinsetedgeFinsetedgeFinset and by invariance of the edge count under a graph isomorphism.

Description

Every colouring of the ℓℓℓ-tuples over a large enough linearly ordered finite set with kkk colours admits a subset of size sss on which the colour of a tuple depends only on its order type: the finite Ramsey theorem for hypergraphs of Erdős and Rado, in the order-type form.

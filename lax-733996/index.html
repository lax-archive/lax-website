      Abstract

      We prove NL=coNL\mathrm{NL}=\mathrm{coNL}NL=coNL by inductive counting on finite configuration graphs. The proof constructs a nondeterministic Turing machine that generates paths and census witnesses sequentially, halts on every branch, and uses logarithmic space. The argument includes correctness of the count sequence and space bounds for the compiled counting machine. Machine classes come from lax-434930.

      Abstract

      We prove NL=coNL\mathrm{NL}=\mathrm{coNL}NL=coNL by inductive counting on finite configuration graphs. The proof constructs a nondeterministic Turing machine that generates paths and census witnesses sequentially, halts on every branch, and uses logarithmic space. Machine classes come from lax-434930; configuration encodings and stack routines come from lax-307052.

Relaxing Global Classifier Robustness on Semimetric Spaces

      Abstract

      Covering criteria characterize relaxed robustness on semimetric spaces. For finite totally ordered spaces, boundary criteria characterize triviality, and every (μ,ε)(\mu,\varepsilon)(μ,ε)-robust partition is trivial when ε≥Δδμ+Δ\varepsilon\ge\Delta\delta\mu+\Deltaε≥Δδμ+Δ. Triviality transfers through finite μ\muμ-closed overlapping decompositions. Restriction and union admit counterexamples. Canonical binary floats, sum products, synchronized maximum products, and padded lists provide ordered semimetric constructions.

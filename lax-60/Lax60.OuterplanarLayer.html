      Theorem

      Let UUU be a set of neighbors of a vertex v0v₀v0​, and suppose the graph induced by UUU is outerplanar. The vertices of UUU have positive ambient teaching sets of size at most three which separate every pair whose closed neighborhoods differ in the ambient graph. Witnesses repairing closed twins of the induced graph may lie outside UUU.

      Adding the common anchor v0v₀v0​ gives sets of size at most four and certifies every layer vertex against all vertices outside the closed neighborhood of v0v₀v0​.

      1import Lax60.PlanarGraphs
      2import Lax60.TeachingMaps
      3
      4/-!
      5---
      6title: Outerplanar neighbor layers admit width-three ambient assignments
      7type: theorem
      8---
      9Let `U` be a set of neighbors of a vertex `v₀`, and suppose the graph induced
      10by `U` is outerplanar. The vertices of `U` have positive ambient teaching
      11sets of size at most three which separate every pair whose closed
      12neighborhoods differ in the ambient graph. Witnesses repairing closed twins
      13of the induced graph may lie outside `U`.
      14
      15Adding the common anchor `v₀` gives sets of size at most four and certifies
      16every layer vertex against all vertices outside the closed neighborhood of
      17`v₀`.
      18-/
      19
      20namespace Lax60.OuterplanarLayer
      21
      22open Lax60.PlanarGraphs
      23open Lax60.TeachingMaps
      24
      25universe u
      26
      27/-- A width-three positive assignment separating all ambient concepts in `U`. -/
      28def IsLayerAssignment {V : Type u} (G : SimpleGraph V)
      29 (U : Finset V) (S : TeachingMap V 1) : Prop :=
      30 (∀ w : V, w ∈ U →
      31 (S w).card ≤ 3 ∧
      32 ∀ x ∈ S w, x ∈ closedBall G 1 w) ∧
      33 ∀ w₁ w₂ : V, w₁ ∈ U → w₂ ∈ U →
      34 DistinctConcepts G 1 w₁ w₂ →
      35 Separates G 1 S w₁ w₂
      36
      37/-- Add a common anchor to every teaching set. -/
      38noncomputable def anchoredTeaching {V : Type u} (v₀ : V)
      39 (S : TeachingMap V 1) : TeachingMap V 1 := by
      40 classical
      41 exact fun w => insert v₀ (S w)
      42
      43/-- The outerplanar layer assignment exists and gains external certification when anchored. -/
      44axiom exists_outerplanar_layer_assignment {V : Type u} [Fintype V]
      45 (G : SimpleGraph V) (v₀ : V) (U : Finset V)
      46 (hneighbors : ∀ w ∈ U, G.Adj v₀ w)
      47 (houter : IsOuterplanar (G.induce (↑U : Set V))) :
      48 ∃ S : TeachingMap V 1,
      49 IsLayerAssignment G U S ∧
      50 (∀ w : V, w ∈ U →
      51 (anchoredTeaching v₀ S w).card ≤ 4 ∧
      52 ∀ y ∈ anchoredTeaching v₀ S w, y ∈ closedBall G 1 w) ∧
      53 ∀ w : V, w ∈ U → ∀ x : V, x ∉ closedBall G 1 v₀ →
      54 ∃ y ∈ anchoredTeaching v₀ S w, y ∉ closedBall G 1 x
      55
      56end Lax60.OuterplanarLayer
      57

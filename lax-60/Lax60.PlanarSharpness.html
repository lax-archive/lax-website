      Theorem

      Let WWW be the five-vertex wheel whose center is 000 and whose rim is the four-cycle 1−2−3−4−11-2-3-4-11−2−3−4−1. The graph is planar and its positive radius-one no-clash teaching dimension is at least four: the center must use all four rim vertices to distinguish itself from the four dominated rim concepts.

      2import Lax60.PlanarGraphs

      5/-!
      6---
      7title: Some planar graph has positive NCTD at least four
      8type: theorem
      9---
      10Let `W` be the five-vertex wheel whose center is `0` and whose rim is the
      11four-cycle `1-2-3-4-1`. The graph is planar and its positive radius-one
      12no-clash teaching dimension is at least four: the center must use all four
      13rim vertices to distinguish itself from the four dominated rim concepts.
      14-/
      15
      16namespace Lax60.PlanarSharpness
      17
      18open Lax60.PlanarGraphs
      19open Lax60.TeachingMaps
      20
      21/-- The five-vertex wheel with a four-cycle rim. -/
      22def sharpPlanarGraph : SimpleGraph (Fin 5) :=
      23 SimpleGraph.edge 0 1 ⊔
      24 SimpleGraph.edge 0 2 ⊔
      25 SimpleGraph.edge 0 3 ⊔
      26 SimpleGraph.edge 0 4 ⊔
      27 SimpleGraph.edge 1 2 ⊔
      28 SimpleGraph.edge 2 3 ⊔
      29 SimpleGraph.edge 3 4 ⊔
      30 SimpleGraph.edge 4 1
      31
      32/-- The five-vertex wheel is planar and has positive NCTD at least four. -/
      33axiom positiveNCTD_ge_four :
      34 IsPlanar sharpPlanarGraph ∧
      35 4 ≤ positiveNCTD sharpPlanarGraph 1
      36
      37end Lax60.PlanarSharpness
